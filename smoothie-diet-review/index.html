The Smoothie Diet Review: What's Inside the 21-Day Program

An honest look at what you get, who it suits, and what to keep in mind before buying.

Affiliate disclosure: if you buy through our links we may earn a commission at no extra cost to you. We did not receive the product for free.

What it is

The Smoothie Diet is a downloadable 21-day program by health coach Drew Sgoutas. It replaces two meals a day with smoothies and keeps one regular meal plus snacks. You get the main guide, over 36 smoothie recipes, a shopping list for each week and a prep guide, plus two bonuses: a quick-start guide and a 3-day smoothie plan.

What we like

  • Recipes are simple and use regular grocery-store ingredients.
  • Weekly shopping lists remove the planning work, which is where many people give up.
  • Instant PDF access; the printable quick-start guide is handy in the kitchen.
  • A 60-day money-back window, per the sales page.

What to keep in mind

  • The sales page uses strong weight-loss language and testimonials. Individual results vary a lot, and no recipe program can guarantee a specific outcome.
  • Replacing two meals a day with smoothies is a big change. If you are pregnant, diabetic, take medication or have any health condition, speak with your doctor first.
  • It's a recipe plan, not a nutrition coaching service.

Who it suits

People who already enjoy smoothies, want a ready-made schedule and shopping lists, and prefer following a plan over improvising.

Who should skip it

Anyone looking for medical guidance, or who dislikes cold drinks and blended meals. Our free guides on freezer packs and breakfast smoothies are a good way to test whether the routine fits you.

Our verdict

A tidy, beginner-friendly smoothie plan

Good value if you want structure and shopping lists. Check the current price and refund terms on the official page.
