Smoothie Freezer Packs: Prep a Week of Breakfasts in 30 Minutes

Bag the fruit, greens and seeds on Sunday, add liquid and blend on weekday mornings.

This post contains affiliate links. Learn more.

Freezer packs remove the morning decision-making. Everything except the liquid goes in a bag; in the morning you dump, pour and blend.

What goes in the pack

  • Fruit (fresh fruit freezes fine in the pack, chopped)
  • Greens (spinach and kale freeze well)
  • Oats, seeds, cocoa, spices
  • Nut butter: a spoonful on a square of parchment works

What stays out

Milk, yogurt and juice. Add them at blending time. Yogurt can be frozen in ice-cube trays and added as cubes, which also thickens the smoothie.

The 30-minute Sunday routine

  1. Line up 7 zip bags or reusable silicone bags. Label with the recipe and date.
  2. Wash and chop all fruit at once.
  3. Assembly-line it: greens in every bag, then fruit, then extras.
  4. Press out the air, lay flat and freeze.

Storage time

Use within about 3 months for best flavor. Flat bags stack like files and thaw a little faster if your blender struggles.

Tip for weak blenders

Let the pack sit on the counter for 5 minutes, and always add liquid first.
