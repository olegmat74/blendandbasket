20 Fruit and Vegetable Smoothie Combinations (A Cheat Sheet)

Which veg hides in which fruit: a pairing chart for spinach, kale, carrot, beet, cucumber and more.

This post contains affiliate links. Learn more.

Adding vegetables to smoothies is easy when you pair them with fruit that covers their flavor. Use this as a cheat sheet.

Mild vegetables (start here)

  • Spinach: banana, mango, pineapple, any berry
  • Cucumber: green apple, lime, mint, pineapple
  • Zucchini (frozen): blueberry, chocolate, peanut butter
  • Cauliflower (frozen): strawberry, banana, vanilla

Stronger vegetables

  • Kale: pineapple, orange, banana, ginger
  • Carrot: orange, mango, ginger, cinnamon
  • Beet (cooked): cherry, raspberry, cocoa
  • Celery: apple, lemon, pear
  • Sweet potato (cooked): banana, cinnamon, maple
  • Pumpkin: banana, pumpkin spice, yogurt
