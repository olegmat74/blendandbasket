How to Make a Thick Smoothie (That You Can Eat With a Spoon)

Frozen everything, less liquid, the tamper trick, and the ingredients that thicken without ice.

This post contains affiliate links. Learn more.

Runny smoothies are almost always a ratio problem. Here's how to fix it.

1. Freeze the fruit

Ice waters a smoothie down as it melts. Frozen fruit gives the same chill with all the flavor. Frozen banana is the creamiest base of all.

2. Start with less liquid

Begin with ½ cup and add a splash at a time. You can always add liquid; you can't take it out.

3. Add a thickener

  • Greek yogurt or frozen yogurt cubes
  • Rolled oats
  • Chia seeds (thicken more as they sit)
  • Avocado
  • Frozen cauliflower rice or zucchini

4. Use the tamper (or stop and stir)

Thick blends stall around the blade. Use the tamper, or stop, stir and restart on low.

5. For bowls, go even thicker

Smoothie bowls need about half the usual liquid. Top with granola, sliced fruit and seeds, and eat right away.
