15 Healthy Breakfast Ideas for Busy Mornings (5 Minutes or Less)

Smoothies, yogurt bowls, egg muffins and toast toppings you can make before the coffee finishes.

A good breakfast doesn't need a recipe card. These ideas all take five minutes or less, or are made ahead.

Blend

  1. Freezer-pack smoothie (prep on Sunday).
  2. Smoothie bowl: same smoothie, less liquid, toppings on top.
  3. Coffee-banana breakfast shake.

Spoon

  1. Greek yogurt, berries, granola, drizzle of honey.
  2. Overnight oats in a jar.
  3. Chia pudding made the night before.
  4. Cottage cheese with peaches and cinnamon.

Grab

  1. Egg muffins baked on Sunday (eggs, spinach, peppers, cheese).
  2. Hard-boiled eggs and fruit.
  3. Homemade oat-banana breakfast cookies.
  4. A wrap with hummus, egg and greens.

Toast

  1. Avocado, lemon and chili flakes.
  2. Peanut butter, banana slices and cinnamon.
  3. Ricotta and berries.
  4. Smashed white beans, olive oil and tomato.

Make the night before easier

Set out the blender, the bowl or the jar at night. Removing one step in the morning is often what makes a habit stick.
