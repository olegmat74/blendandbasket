Meal Prep for Beginners: A Simple Sunday Plan for the Whole Week

Prep components, not complete meals: a grain, a protein, two vegetables, a sauce and breakfast.

This post contains affiliate links. Learn more.

Beginners often try to cook five identical lunches and are sick of them by Wednesday. Prepping components instead gives you mix-and-match meals all week.

The 2-hour component plan

  • A grain: a pot of rice, quinoa or farro.
  • A protein: a sheet pan of chicken thighs, baked tofu or a batch of lentils.
  • Two vegetables: one roasted (broccoli, sweet potato), one raw (cucumber, slaw mix).
  • A sauce: yogurt-herb, peanut-lime or a simple vinaigrette.
  • Breakfast: smoothie freezer packs or overnight oats.

Order of operations

  1. Oven on. Vegetables and protein onto sheet pans.
  2. Grain on the stove.
  3. While those cook: wash greens, chop raw veg, make the sauce.
  4. Assemble breakfast packs.
  5. Cool everything before lidding the containers.

How long it keeps

Most cooked components are best eaten within 3–4 days in the fridge, so plan a mini-prep on Wednesday or freeze half.

Containers

Glass containers with snap lids reheat well; a few deli containers are perfect for sauces.
