Green Smoothies for Beginners: 5 Recipes That Don't Taste Like Lawn

Start mild with spinach, lean on frozen fruit, and build up from there. Our five starter ratios.

This post contains affiliate links. Learn more.

Most people quit green smoothies after one gritty, bitter glass. The fix is simple: start with mild greens, use plenty of frozen fruit, and blend in the right order.

The starter ratio

For one large glass: 1 packed cup of baby spinach + 1½ cups frozen fruit + 1 cup liquid. Spinach is almost tasteless once blended with banana or mango. Kale is stronger; save it for week two.

Blend in this order

  1. Liquid first (milk, oat milk, water or coconut water).
  2. Greens next. Blend 20 seconds until smooth.
  3. Frozen fruit last, then blend 30–45 seconds.

Blending greens with the liquid first is what removes the leafy bits.

Five starter recipes

  • Tropical: spinach, frozen mango, frozen pineapple, coconut water, squeeze of lime.
  • Peanut butter banana: spinach, frozen banana, 1 tbsp peanut butter, milk, pinch of cinnamon.
  • Berry green: spinach, mixed frozen berries, half a banana, Greek yogurt, water.
  • Apple pie: spinach, half an apple, frozen banana, rolled oats, almond milk, cinnamon.
  • Mint chip: spinach, frozen banana, a few mint leaves, milk, 1 tsp cacao nibs on top.

Level up slowly

Once these taste normal, swap a quarter of the spinach for kale, then try cucumber or celery. Add one new ingredient at a time so you know what you like.
