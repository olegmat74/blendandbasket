High-Protein Smoothies Without Protein Powder

Greek yogurt, cottage cheese, tofu, milk and seeds: how much protein each adds, and 6 recipes.

This post contains affiliate links. Learn more.

Protein powder works, but plenty of people dislike the taste or would rather use whole foods. These common ingredients add real protein (approximate amounts per serving):

  • Greek yogurt, ¾ cup: about 15–17 g
  • Cottage cheese, ½ cup: about 12–14 g
  • Milk, 1 cup: about 8 g (soy milk is similar; almond and oat are much lower)
  • Silken tofu, ½ cup: about 9–10 g
  • Hemp seeds, 3 tbsp: about 9–10 g
  • Peanut butter, 2 tbsp: about 7 g

Check your own labels; brands vary.

Six recipes

  1. Strawberry cheesecake: cottage cheese, frozen strawberries, milk, graham cracker crumbs on top.
  2. Chocolate PB: Greek yogurt, cocoa, peanut butter, banana, milk.
  3. Tofu berry: silken tofu, mixed berries, soy milk, honey.
  4. Hemp green: hemp seeds, spinach, pineapple, banana, water.
  5. Vanilla chai: Greek yogurt, chai spices, frozen banana, milk.
  6. Orange creamsicle: cottage cheese, orange segments, frozen mango, vanilla.

Texture tip

Cottage cheese turns silky once blended for a full 45 seconds. Nobody will spot it.
