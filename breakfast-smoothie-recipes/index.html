10 Breakfast Smoothies That Actually Keep You Full Until Lunch

Fruit alone is a snack. Add protein, fat and fiber and a smoothie becomes breakfast. Ten combos.

This post contains affiliate links. Learn more.

A glass of blended fruit is pleasant, but on its own it tends to leave you hungry by ten. A breakfast smoothie needs three things fruit doesn't bring: protein, some fat and fiber.

The breakfast formula

  • Protein: Greek yogurt, cottage cheese, milk, silken tofu, or a scoop of protein powder.
  • Fat: nut butter, half an avocado, chia, flax or hemp seeds.
  • Fiber: rolled oats, berries, chia, a handful of greens.
  • Fruit: about 1 cup, mostly frozen for texture.

Ten combinations

  1. Blueberry oat: blueberries, oats, Greek yogurt, milk, vanilla.
  2. PB&J: strawberries, peanut butter, oats, milk.
  3. Coffee banana: cold brew, frozen banana, almond butter, cocoa.
  4. Carrot cake: grated carrot, banana, oats, cinnamon, walnuts, milk.
  5. Cherry almond: frozen cherries, almond butter, yogurt, almond milk.
  6. Peach pie: frozen peaches, cottage cheese, oats, nutmeg.
  7. Chocolate raspberry: raspberries, cocoa, silken tofu, oat milk.
  8. Pumpkin spice: canned pumpkin, banana, yogurt, pumpkin pie spice.
  9. Green apple: spinach, apple, hemp seeds, kefir.
  10. Mango lassi style: mango, plain yogurt, cardamom, milk.

Make it a habit

Pick two favorites per week and buy for only those. Rotating 14 recipes sounds fun but fills the fridge with half-used ingredients.
