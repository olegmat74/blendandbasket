A Healthy Grocery List on a Budget: $60 for a Week of Meals

The cheap staples that do the heavy lifting, with a sample week and a few store-brand swaps.

This post contains affiliate links. Learn more.

Eating well on a tight budget comes down to building meals around inexpensive staples and using everything you buy. Prices vary by region; this list is a starting framework for one or two people.

Staples

  • Oats, rice, dry or canned beans, lentils
  • Eggs
  • Bananas, apples, carrots, cabbage, onions
  • Frozen vegetables and frozen fruit
  • Canned tomatoes and tuna
  • Peanut butter, plain yogurt, milk

A sample week

  • Breakfast: banana-oat smoothies or overnight oats.
  • Lunch: bean and rice bowls with cabbage slaw.
  • Dinner: lentil soup, egg fried rice with frozen veg, tuna pasta with tomatoes.
  • Snacks: apples with peanut butter, carrots, yogurt.

Five money savers

  1. Buy frozen fruit for smoothies; it's often cheaper than fresh out of season.
  2. Choose store brands for staples.
  3. Shop with a list and plan around what's on sale.
  4. Cook one big pot twice a week.
  5. Freeze ripe bananas and leftover greens instead of tossing them.
