The Smoothie Grocery List: What to Buy for a Week of Blending

A printable-style list split into frozen, fresh, pantry and dairy, with rough quantities for one person.

This post contains affiliate links. Learn more.

Buying for smoothies is different from buying for meals: frozen fruit is your base, and a few pantry items carry the flavor. Here is our one-person, seven-smoothie list.

Frozen aisle

  • Mixed berries, 2 lb bag
  • Mango chunks, 1 lb
  • Pineapple or cherries, 1 lb
  • Optional: frozen cauliflower rice (adds creaminess, no taste)

Produce

  • Bananas, 5–6 (freeze the ripe ones peeled, in halves)
  • Baby spinach, 1 large tub
  • 1 lemon or lime
  • 1 cucumber or a few celery stalks if you like green flavors

Dairy or alternatives

  • Milk or oat/almond milk, half a gallon
  • Plain Greek yogurt, 32 oz tub

Pantry

  • Rolled oats
  • Peanut or almond butter
  • Chia or ground flax
  • Cinnamon, vanilla, unsweetened cocoa

Saving money

Store-brand frozen fruit is usually as good as name brands. Buy bananas on sale, let them ripen, and freeze them: overripe bananas make the sweetest smoothies.
